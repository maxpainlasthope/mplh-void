MPLH : VOID

TWO UNIVERSES. ONE VOID.

Explore a deep-space experience built around two independent game modes. In Void Hook, you recover drifting objects with a precision tether system. In Void Strike, you pilot a fighter through dangerous sectors and fast arcade combat.

Two distinct space adventures

Each mode has its own feel, progression and challenges, while sharing the same universe and visual identity.

Mode 01

VOID HOOK

Take aim with a futuristic tether, retrieve valuable objects and beat the mission target before the timer runs out.

  • Precision hook-and-recover gameplay
  • Upgrade-focused progression
  • Space salvage atmosphere
Mode 02

VOID STRIKE

Fly into hostile sectors, manage your Hull, weapons and movement, and push deeper into a fast-paced starfighter challenge.

  • Arcade-style combat runs
  • Fighter and weapon collection
  • Progressive enemy pressure
Core highlights

Built around style, progression and replay value

The project focuses on original presentation, clean sci-fi identity and repeatable play sessions with long-term unlock goals.

✦

Original 3D content

Stylized fighters, weapons and space objects give the project a consistent and recognizable visual language.

◎

Independent systems

Void Hook and Void Strike do not depend on each other, allowing each mode to develop its own rules and difficulty curve.

∞

Long-term progression

Collect resources, unlock equipment and keep improving your play across repeated sessions.
